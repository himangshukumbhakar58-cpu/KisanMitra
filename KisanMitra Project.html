The problem

Expertise is out of reach

Agricultural extension officers are scarce, and most farming information online is scattered, technical, and in English — hard to use for someone who needs a quick, practical answer in the field.

Who it's for

Smallholder farmers

Marginal and smallholder farmers in West Bengal — paddy and jute growers, among others — who are far more comfortable asking a question in Bengali or Hindi than searching the web in English.

Why Gemini

Conversation, not documents

Gemini understands vague, plain-language descriptions ("leaves turning yellow") and responds conversationally in the farmer's own language — something no static FAQ or search engine can do.
